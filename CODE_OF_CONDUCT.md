# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people. Respect privacy and attribution. Do not publish another person's private information, use sexualized or discriminatory language, harass others, or disrupt the community.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, harmful, or inconsistent with these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer contact. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect privacy where possible, and avoid conflicts of interest.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1. For the full community standard, see [contributor-covenant.org](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
